## Selected projects & research

### EPIFAT — medical image segmentation & radiomics
Automated segmentation and quantitative analysis of epicardial adipose tissue in cardiac CT. The work combines deep-learning segmentation, medical-image processing, radiomic feature extraction, desktop tooling, and reproducible evaluation.

### Tensor-based modal decomposition
Tensor methods for reconstruction and forecasting of high-dimensional spatio-temporal fields from sparse measurements, including low-rank decompositions and sensor-placement strategies.

### RAG & LLM systems
Retrieval pipelines, deterministic chunking, hybrid search, reranking, grounded generation, evaluation, observability, and production-facing APIs for domain knowledge assistants.

### Document intelligence
LLM-assisted grammar checking, OCR/document parsing, provenance-aware processing, and automated verification pipelines for structured and unstructured documents.
